chapter 03 — the system

the tools to keep it tight

Four colors. Four fonts. That's the whole brand. Miss the green? Wrong brand. Rock Salt on the tagline only — always with a glow, always a little crooked, never precious.

voice & usage 04 / 04
do

Lead with the mouth. "Plant Bitch" belongs on the wordmark — they're a package deal, not separate marks.

don't

Never recolor the neon pink. Pink is the voice; muting it kills the whole joke.

do

Keep one leaf-green accent in every layout — the green is what says "this is a plant brand, not a nightclub."

don't

Don't rotate the wordmark. The only thing diagonal in this brand is the Rock Salt tagline.
