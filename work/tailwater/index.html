the project

three homes on a river-side street.

Tailwater is a boutique collection of three custom homes bordering Hop Porter Park in Old Hailey, Idaho — footsteps from the Big Wood River and a walkable downtown. Developed by Raised Bar Development Group, the goal was to build homes that felt equally at home for a young family, a second-home buyer, or a multi-generational household.

My job was to make the pre-construction pitch look as good as the finished product would. That meant developing every home's exterior, interior, and ADU as marketing-ready photorealism — the kind of imagery that carries a listing, a landing page, and a buyer conversation without the buyer needing to squint.

the three homes

each one different. each one Tailwater.

Same street, same landscape, three distinct plans. The Della and The Big Lost each pair a full main house with an attached ADU — perfect for rental income or multi-gen living. The Carbonate is the design statement of the trio, a more compact plan with split-limestone and window colonnades as its signature exterior move.
