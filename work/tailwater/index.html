the brief

renderings that sell homes, not just show them.

Raised Bar Ventures is developing a residential community in Hailey, Idaho, with three distinct home model concepts and multi-unit deployments. They needed marketing-ready renderings that could carry listings, presentations, and buyer conversations — photorealistic enough to close, cinematic enough to remember.

The scope covered exteriors for the flagship Tailwater build, three home model concept renderings (The Della, Carbonate, and The Big Lost), and full interior visualization for Lot 1 — main home plus ADU. Every space, every angle, developed from the architectural files and delivered ready to publish.
