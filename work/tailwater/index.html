Multi-home development

Residential visualization developed from existing architectural models for a multi-home development in Hailey, Idaho. The goal was marketing-ready imagery that could carry a listing on its own — interiors people could imagine living in, exteriors that read as photography, not renders.

Camera direction, materials, lighting, furniture and styling, and final image production all handled in-house from the same architectural files the builder was already working from.
